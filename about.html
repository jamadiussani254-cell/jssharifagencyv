My Story

Good marketing starts with a clear plan: who you want to reach, where they spend their time, and what you want them to do next.

As a Google Ads PPC specialist, I focus on putting your business in front of people at the moment they search for what you offer. Around that, I run Facebook & Instagram Ads and Social Media Marketing to build awareness and bring people back, and YouTube SEO to help your videos get found.

I work across Google Ads PPC, Social Media Marketing, YouTube SEO, Facebook & Instagram Ads and Digital Marketing Strategy. You might need one of these or several working together. Either way, the aim is a plan that fits your business and your budget.
